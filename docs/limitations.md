# Limitations

- Olist is historical Brazilian data (2016–2018), not a current delivery feed or evidence about Indonesia.
- Completed-delivery model evaluation is subject to selection bias; it does not predict canceled or lost orders.
- The triage calculator models assumptions and cannot establish causal intervention ROI.
- The AWS replay and reconciliation are complete, but the dashboard is not publicly hosted and Power BI authoring remains optional/manual.
